How these get built

Raw

Ingest the source exactly as it arrives — untyped, occasionally malformed, no assumptions made about cleanliness yet.

Validated

Type, dedupe, and test. Nulls, duplicates, and referential breaks get named and either fixed or documented as expected.

Modeled

Shape into facts and dimensions someone can actually query — with the history (SCD2, where it matters) preserved correctly.
